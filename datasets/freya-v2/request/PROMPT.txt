DATASET REQUEST v2 — BANKING AND INSURANCE, WITHIN-SECTOR GENERALIZATION
This replaces the v1 request. Read this file first, then both specification files.

We are building a program that reads an unfamiliar business brief and tool schema
and creates a Freya phone workflow. We will use GEPA to improve the builder using
training evidence. We need to measure interpretation of new policies and robust
conversation handling, not memorization of 13 public collections customers.

SCOPE
Focus on BANKING AND INSURANCE. Do not expand into file management, retail,
hospitality or arbitrary industries. Generalizable means reading unfamiliar rules,
exceptions, terminology, data structures and conversation situations within these
two sectors. Collections is one development example, not a rule to bake into every
generated agent. No domain-specific action is universally required: its authority,
preconditions, confirmation, disclosure and closing rules come from the supplied
brief and tool contract.

The kickoff's generalization comments and the written final rules are separate
evidence. Read sources/freya-scope-notes.txt. Current written final rules describe
new briefs for the same kind of call, with the same seven bank tool names but
potentially different fields and enums. Broader banking/insurance tasks below are
our LOCAL robustness tests, not a claim about announced hidden-test contents.

DELIVER TWO CLEARLY SEPARATED DATASETS
1. live/: 48 additional customers supported by the actual 13 bank fixtures.
   Follow LIVE_CASES_SPEC.txt. These are candidates for Freya practice execution.
2. generalization/: 6 completely synthetic, self-contained banking/insurance brief
   bundles with tool contracts and 4 customer cases each. Follow
   GENERALIZATION_SPEC.txt. These require a LOCAL MOCK implementation; they cannot
   be run by pretending to be a registered Freya practice customer.

Use downloadable JSON/text files, preferably packaged in a ZIP. Do not return only
a plan, a taxonomy or fenced snippets. If supported tools let you create files and
validate them with code, do that. Report honestly what you checked. Never claim
that you called Freya, ran our builder or measured success.

CONVERSATION DIMENSIONS: OPEN-ENDED, NOT A CLOSED SENTIMENT LIST
Treat these as independent dimensions that can change during a conversation:
- Affect/tone: examples include calm, satisfied, frustrated, anxious, embarrassed,
  impatient, relieved, disappointed, hesitant, suspicious, sarcastic or enthusiastic.
  These are examples, not an exhaustive enum. Do not invent diagnoses or infer an
  identity or financial fact from affect.
- Actual intent: asking a question, accepting, refusing, correcting, postponing,
  requesting a human, changing scope, expressing inability, or disputing a fact.
- Cooperation/knowledge: informed or confused, terse or expansive, uncertain about
  a detail, distracted, willingly cooperative or reluctant to share information.
- Information delivery: fragments, self-correction, interruptions, negation plus
  an alternative, references such as "the other one", words for dates/numbers,
  several intents in one turn and a question inserted into a requested answer.
- Trust and adversarial behavior: legitimate fraud concerns, inaccurate claims,
  impersonation or attempts to override instructions/authorization. Prompt injection
  is not an emotion. A skeptical or angry customer is not automatically malicious.

Include emotional transitions, such as skeptical to reassured or cooperative to
frustrated after a repeated question. Do not force all axes into every case. Most
cases should combine one or two difficulties; a minority can be compound stress
cases. Include ordinary successful controls and benign statements that resemble
an attack but merely ask about the process. A defensive agent that rejects every
customer must perform poorly on this dataset.

EXPECTED TRANSFERABLE CAPABILITIES
- Keep decision, question, correction and tone separate; preserve simultaneous
  legitimate requests and policy-required priority.
- Ground state changes in current evidence. Do not treat an omitted field as a
  repeated value, an old value as new consent, or unknown as a negative disposition.
- Keep each object's state separate. A change to one account/policy/request must
  not silently change another. Ground references in actual supplied tool data.
- Distinguish intent to do something, a claim it happened, authorization to do it,
  the attempted tool call and a successful result. A question is not consent.
- Derive dates, amounts, retry limits, identity requirements and closure outcomes
  from the current brief, clock and tool contract. Never assume the public brief's
  values or even the presence of its business rules in another task.
- Follow the supplied scope/precedence of amendments. Do not combine a superseded
  general rule with its replacement or apply a scoped exception globally.
- Recover from clarification, changed terms and supported tool failures without
  duplicating irreversible actions or claiming a failed action succeeded.

SOURCES AND DATA SAFETY
sources/ contains official kit v3 README, Brief A, Brief B, OpenAPI, 13 public
synthetic cases, and clearly attributed Freya-only kickoff excerpts/notes.
Use the official documents for live-case ground truth. Generalization policies
must be explicitly labeled synthetic and be self-contained. Do not pass an
invented policy off as a Freya rule. Do not use real customer data, credentials,
web-scraped datasets, hidden briefs or guesses about hidden answers.

SPLITS AND NON-LEAKAGE
Training examples feed the optimizer. Validation controls candidate selection.
Holdout is opened only after selection and is never used for reflection or repair.
Keep all paraphrases, policy counterfactuals and variants of the same scenario
family together. Varying a name or number does not create an independent family.
The live set measures new behavior under known briefs. The generalization set
measures new brief families; holdout brief families must be absent from training.
Do not summarize holdout scripts, unique rules or expected answers in shared
review files. Shared manifests may list IDs, paths, counts and split membership.

OUTPUT ROOT
- live/train.json, live/validation.json, live/holdout.json
- live/manifest.json
- generalization/{train,validation,holdout}/<bundle-id>/...
- generalization/manifest.json
- review.txt: actual checks, limitations and unresolved issues, without holdout
  contents. Put holdout-specific issues inside the holdout directory.

QUALITY OVER QUANTITY
Return fewer defensible cases rather than fabricating unsupported behavior to fill
a quota. Flag unresolved policy ambiguity instead of making up an oracle. For each
case, expected outcomes must follow its customer behavior, applicable rules and
tool responses. Validate machine-readable files and source references before
returning them. All examples are proposed tests, not official scores.
